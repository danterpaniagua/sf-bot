## Current working theory

Most of September's noise and the lack of notifications come from alert configuration, not from the services: tag/action mismatches left whole families (MOBI, ARCA, SSL "No data") undelivered; tier chains were inverted so only the lowest tier could surface; windows and thresholds were too short or too low (single-minute `Caída brusca` dips, a 9 req/s Warning at MOBI-02's normal peak, a 45-day certificate tier that fires on every healthy renewal); and the stock IIS queue trigger never evaluated. The one real capacity event in September (2026-09-04, MOBI-02 CPU saturation) was caught late by the old 3-minute/10-minute mix and only by the native CPU alert. Treat the delivery changes as plausible until the first real MOBI or ARCA alert reaches the Operaciones space.

## Ruled out

- "Thresholds are hardcoded in the trigger expressions" — artifact of `expandExpression`; the raw expressions reference macros.
- "One global Google webhook" — each user's media carries its own Chat path; Operaciones and the leaders use different spaces.
- "`scope:OPE` + `LOYALTY` + `action ~ MAIL` routes via action 10" — the live condition is `EMAIL`.
- "Telegram alerts come from these Zabbix actions" — media type 16 is unconfigured and unused.
- "The three daytime `Mobile API Login` failures were a total traffic block" — traffic was normal.
- "A September episode of `Caída brusca` was a WAF block" — every candidate has another explanation (burst end, host start, nightly ramp-down).
- "SFDEV02 would page Operaciones about a dev host" — the host is disabled.
- "The collapse detector can watch MOBI-01" — since 2026-05-12 it idles at a ~0.07 req/s floor; the detector on its own item gave 24 recurring false episodes.
- "The old cross-host sum item can feed the detector" — it adds each host's stale last value when MOBI-02 is off; replaced by a calculated item that counts MOBI-02 only while it reports.
- "A MOBI-02-only detector is enough" — it would misread a return of the traffic to MOBI-01; the service-level detector replaces it (the MOBI-02-only trigger 26287 is kept disabled as a fallback).
