## Capacity estimate (arithmetic on the numbers above)

- OpenSearch disk watermarks, if the cluster uses the defaults (not yet read from the cluster): low 85%, high 90%, flood stage 95%. At flood stage OpenSearch puts a read-only-allow-delete block on indices that have a shard on that node, which on a single node stops Graylog from indexing new messages until space is freed and the block is lifted. Percentages of 512 GB: 85% = 435 GB (already passed), 90% = 461 GB (6.8 GB away), 95% = 486 GB (32 GB away).
- At the reported ingest rates, the time to each level from 454 GB used: 21 GB/day gives about 7.8 hours to 90%, about 1.5 days to 95%, about 2.8 days to 100%; 24 GB/day gives about 6.8 hours, 1.35 days and 2.5 days. These ignore anything that deletes data in the meantime (index rotation and retention), whose settings have not been read, so they are the worst case with no reclaim.
- The high watermark would be crossed within hours on a single node; relocation has nowhere to go, so the visible effect is mainly the flood-stage block later. Timing of the nightly or hourly rotation, if any, would change these estimates.

### Which accounts fail and who calls (Warning lines, `data/business/detail-warn-*`, gitignored; pull of 2026-10-09 18:05 local)
- The wallet `userId` of `GET /api/v1/WalletCredentials/{userId}` is the numeric MercadoPago `WalletUserId` (code comment in `WalletCredentialsService`), not an Auth0 `sub`; the tokens live in the Business database (`WalletAccounts`, `WalletCredentials`: encrypted access and refresh tokens, `Status`, `ExpiresAt`, `LinkedAt`) and are refreshed directly against MercadoPago. Auth0 does not hold them, so an Auth0 lookup would not show this grant.
- 5 wallet ids request the endpoint (about 528k, 331k, 150k, 60k and 21k requests per day since 10-08). In a 44-second sample of 200 Warning lines (5,301 Warnings in the previous 10 minutes), `WalletCredentials: refresh failed` appeared for only 2 of them: 120 for one id with credential 31 and 80 for another with credential 66, both `applicationType=QR`, `mpStatusCode=400`, `mpErrorCode=invalid_grant`, the request asking for `applications=Delivery,QR`. The two failing ids are the two with the most requests (about 860k per day against about 760k errors per day), so the other three are probably succeeding; this was not verified over a longer window. This narrows the earlier "about 5 accounts" to 2 confirmed.
- Code (branch `main` @ d969856fa, working tree): on `MpApiException` `ProcessCredentialAsync` logs the Warning and returns `null` without changing the credential's state (code comment: "NO se transiciona el estado"), and `RequiresRenewal()` is `ExpiresAt <= now + 7 days`. A credential whose refresh token is rejected therefore stays Active and in the renewal window, and every request retries the same refresh. This explains the repetition and the Warning plus Error pair. The staggered start (22:30 on 10-07, then steps until 23:00 on 10-08) could match each credential entering its 7-day window; hypothesis, testable with `scripts-wallet-credentials.sql` (not run).
- Caller: the `UserId` property of the Warning lines is an Auth0 machine-to-machine subject (`<client_id>@clients`). That same `client_id` appears as a literal in the production configuration of both SmartPedidos services, Concentrador (`develop` @ 01494650) and Platform (`develop` @ 373144e), so either can be the caller; the token does not tell them apart. Both failing wallet ids also appear in the Concentrador's `mercadopago_token_fetch failed` / `mercadopago_token_invalid_response` errors of GITIN-2208.
