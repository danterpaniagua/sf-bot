## Proposed design (nothing built)
- For each of the two PROD healthchecks, on host `Zabbix Proxy`:
  - High: `count(rspcode,#3,"ne",200)=3 or nodata(rspcode,{$HC.NODATA.WINDOW})=1` (three consecutive non-200 answers, or no answer for the window; detection in about 1.5 min for MobileApp and 3 min for WebServiceV2).
  - Warning: `avg(time,5m) > {$HC.RESP.WARN}` with recovery `avg(time,5m) < {$HC.RESP.WARN.RECOVERY}`.
  - Macros (host `Zabbix Proxy`): `{$HC.NODATA.WINDOW}` = 3m, `{$HC.RESP.WARN}` = 2 (s; about 3.6 times the observed normal; one 11 s spike among ten 30 s samples gives an average of about 1.5 s, below the threshold), `{$HC.RESP.WARN.RECOVERY}` = 1.6.
  - Tags for Loyalty routing: `scope=OPE`, `scope=GIT-SML-OPE`, `service_group=LOYALTY`, `action=GCHAT` (action 7 needs `scope = GIT-SML-OPE` and `action ~ GCHAT` and `service_group` LOYALTY or CLOUD).
- The two existing WebServiceV2 triggers stay untouched until the user decides (disable once the new ones are confirmed).

## Design accepted (user, 2026-10-15: "Proposed accepted. values in macros")
- The proposed design is accepted as written; all thresholds go in macros. `scripts/scripts-c2.sh` (written, syntax-checked, not run) implements it with dry / apply / rollback modes.
- Differences from the proposal, made so every value is a macro: the High condition is "at least `{$HC.FAIL.MIN}` (3) non-200 answers in the last `{$HC.FAIL.WINDOW}` (3m), or no data for `{$HC.NODATA.WINDOW}` (3m)" instead of "three consecutive" (a time-window count; for MobileApp every 30 s that is 6 samples, for WebServiceV2 every minute it is 3, so all three must fail); `#N` cannot be a macro in the same way, so the count is by time window. Warning: `avg(time, {$HC.RESP.WINDOW}=5m) > {$HC.RESP.WARN}=2` s, recovery below `{$HC.RESP.WARN.RECOVERY}=1.6` s; each Warning trigger depends on the High trigger of the same healthcheck.
- Tags: `scope=OPE`, `scope=GIT-SML-OPE`, `service_group=LOYALTY`, `action=GCHAT`, `check=healthcheck`, `service=<scenario>`. Existing triggers 26860 and 26861 are not touched.
- Unverified: that Zabbix 6.0.32 accepts the `"ne"` operator and macros as the period in `count()`, `avg()` and `nodata()` (the script stops at the first trigger if the server refuses).
